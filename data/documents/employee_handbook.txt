NOVA CORP — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Company Overview
Nova Corp is a technology company focused on artificial intelligence, data science, software engineering, cloud computing, and enterprise solutions.

Working Hours
Regular working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees are expected to arrive on time and maintain their scheduled working hours.

Attendance
Employees must record their attendance using the company's attendance system. Repeated late arrivals or unauthorized absences may affect performance evaluations.

Employee Benefits
Eligible employees may receive health insurance, paid leave, performance bonuses, professional development opportunities, and other benefits according to company policy.

Workplace Conduct
Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior, respect colleagues, protect company information, and follow organizational policies.

Information Security
Employees must protect confidential company information. Passwords and authentication credentials must never be shared with other employees.

Leave
Employees should request planned leave through the official leave management system and obtain approval from their manager before taking leave, except in emergencies.
