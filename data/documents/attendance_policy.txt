NOVA CORP — ATTENDANCE POLICY

Working Schedule
The standard working schedule is Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Attendance Recording
Employees must record their arrival and departure using the designated attendance system.

Late Arrival
Employees arriving after their scheduled start time should provide an appropriate explanation to their manager. Frequent late arrivals may result in corrective action.

Early Departure
Employees who need to leave before the end of their scheduled working hours should obtain approval from their manager whenever possible.

Absence
Employees who cannot attend work should notify their manager and submit the appropriate leave request.

Remote Work
Employees approved for remote work must remain available during scheduled working hours and maintain communication with their team.

Attendance and Performance
Attendance patterns may be considered as part of employee performance reviews.
