NOVA CORP — LEAVE POLICY

Annual Leave
Full-time employees are eligible for annual paid leave according to their employment category and company rules.

Sick Leave
Employees who are unable to work because of illness should notify their manager as soon as possible. Medical documentation may be required for extended periods of sick leave.

Casual Leave
Casual leave may be used for personal matters or unexpected situations. Employees should submit a leave request through the official leave management system.

Leave Approval
Planned leave should normally be submitted at least three working days before the intended leave date. Leave is subject to manager approval and business requirements.

Emergency Leave
Employees may inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible when an emergency prevents them from attending work.

Unauthorized Absence
Absence without approval or appropriate notification may be treated as unauthorized absence and may affect attendance records and performance evaluations.

Leave Records
All approved and rejected leave requests are maintained in the company's leave management system.
